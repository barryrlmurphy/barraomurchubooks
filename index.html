About the author

Stories dug up
rather than written.

Barra Ó Murchú was born in Galway in 1968. He read archaeology at University College Dublin and spent a decade on early-medieval coastal digs — Inishbofin, Skellig Michael, the Lambay middens — before publishing his first novel, The Hostage King, in 2015.

The novels are violent, plainspoken, and unhurried. They are about what gets done to bodies, and what the people who survive remember afterwards.
