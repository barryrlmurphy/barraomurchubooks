The Author

Barra Ó Murchú

b. Galway, MCMLXVIII · five novels, MMXV — MMXXVI

Barra Ó Murchú was born in Galway in 1968. He read archaeology at University College Dublin and spent a decade on early-medieval coastal digs — Inishbofin, Skellig Michael, the Lambay middens — before publishing his first novel, The Hostage King, in 2015.

The novels are set on and around the Irish Sea between roughly the 8th and 11th centuries. They are violent, plainspoken, and unhurried. They do not have heroes, exactly; nor villains. They are about what gets done to bodies, and what the people who survive remember afterwards.

❦

Ó Murchú has written, in The Year Book and elsewhere, that the novels are an attempt to make “a historical fiction in which nobody is consoled by the past, and the past is not consoled by us.” The dig sites, he has said, taught him to write about a place as if it were already gone.

He lives on the Beara peninsula in West Cork with his wife, the historian Ailbhe Ní Fhloinn, and two children. He does not own a telephone. Correspondence is welcome by post or — under protest — through the agency.

❦
