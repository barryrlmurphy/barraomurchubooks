Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 2026

Hi there. I am Barra Ó Murchú, the author behind barraomurchubooks.com. This website is an imprint of Skean Publishing. I value your privacy and believe in keeping things as simple and transparent as possible. This policy explains how I handle your data.

❦

I. Data I Collect (And Why)

  • Newsletter sign-ups. If you choose to subscribe to my newsletter to receive research notes and book-release updates, I collect your email address. I only use this to send you the newsletter.
  • Website analytics. I use basic, anonymised website tracking to see which pages are popular. This does not identify you personally, and is used purely to keep the site running smoothly.

II. Third-party Services

I use trusted, industry-standard services to process your data safely:
  • Email marketing. Your email address is stored securely with my newsletter provider solely for sending you my updates.
  • Web hosting. My website platform securely hosts this site and complies with strict international data-protection laws.

III. Cookies

This site uses basic cookies to ensure it loads quickly and functions properly. You can block cookies via your web-browser settings at any time without hurting your experience on this site.

IV. No Spam, No Selling

I will never sell, rent, trade or share your email address or personal details with anyone else.

V. Your Control — The Right to be Forgotten

You have total control over your data:
  • You can unsubscribe from my newsletter instantly at any time by clicking the “Unsubscribe” link at the foot of any email I send you.
  • If you want your data completely wiped from my records, just email me via the Contact page and I will delete it immediately.

VI. Contact Me

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, you can reach me directly via the Contact page on this website.
