From the back flap

Saltgrave opens on a beach in the spring of 824 with the tide drawing back from a body. Over the days that follow, a young monk who survives the raid on Inishbofin carries his abbot's salted head north along the coast, looking for a Viking he can recognise. It is a short, hard book about grief, the long memory of violence, and what a body weighs when it is being carried somewhere.
