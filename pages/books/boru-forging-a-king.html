From the back flap

IRELAND, 10th CENTURY.

Fractured by blood feuds and rival kings, the island bleeds under the Viking axe. Along the River Shannon, Norse longships bring fire and slavery to the south. In the shattered kingdom of Munster, one man refuses to break.

Brian Boru wants vengeance, not a crown. Outnumbered and hunted, he wages a brutal guerrilla war from the dense forests of Thomond. But a bloodied sword cannot heal a broken land. To crush the Northmen, Brian must conquer his own countrymen first.

Witness the rise of Ireland’s greatest king through the eyes of the allies who bled for him—and the tyrants who tried to break him. The chronicle begins here.
