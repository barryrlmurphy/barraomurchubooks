The Drowned Abbey

A novel of the Shannon

A flood-year on the Shannon. The brothers at Clonmacnoise wait out the rising water with a body in the crypt and a woman they cannot bring themselves to drown.

Buy the book

From the back flap

In the wet winter of 996 the Shannon comes over its banks for nine weeks, and the brothers at Clonmacnoise wait out the flood with a body in the crypt and a young woman they cannot bring themselves to drown. The Drowned Abbey is about silence, weather, and what gets done in a monastery when there is nothing to do but watch a river.

“A novel of weather and silence. Almost unbearable, and very fine.”

— Times Literary Supplement
